Basic information

  • Name: Niya Solutions
  • Type: Partner for project management, facility management, cleaning, and handyman services.
  • Founders: Abdennour El Moussaoui
  • Headquarters: Overijse, Belgium
  • Website: https://www.niya-solutions.be/
  • Category: Facility management, project management, commercial cleaning company, and handyman services.

Background

Niya Solutions was established to relieve businesses across Belgium of operational burdens. Since 2010, the company has focused on supporting organizations with facility management, project management, cleaning, and handyman works. The philosophy of the business is rooted in a personal approach, allowing clients to avoid endless call transfers and unpersonal service by offering direct communication with the business manager. This setup enables quick, efficient decision-making and customized problem-solving, so clients can focus entirely on their core business operations.

Core features & Solutions

  • Facility Management: Actively thinking along to optimize business processes and find tailored solutions for smooth company operations.
  • Project Management: Taking full control of projects from the first consultation to the final completion, ensuring a perfect finish from A to Z.
  • Cleaning Company (Schoonmaakbedrijf): Comprehensive cleaning services catering to diverse environments ranging from corporate offices to industrial facilities.
  • Handyman Services (Klusbedrijf): Carrying out all types of maintenance and repair tasks, whether the job is large or small.
  • Single Point of Contact: Providing a direct line of communication with the business manager, eliminating unnecessary intermediaries for transparent and fast communication.

Ideal for

  • Companies and corporate offices in Belgium looking for a reliable partner to manage facility operations or office cleaning services.
  • Industrial businesses requiring professional, specialized cleaning for factory or industrial environments.
  • Catering industries (horeca), schools, and syndics (syndicussen) seeking long-term cooperation for property maintenance, project management, and daily operational support.
  • Organizations of any size looking to outsource minor or major handyman chores and structural projects to a single, end-to-end provider.

Trust signals & Highlights

  • Established History: Successfully supporting and relieving businesses across Belgium with their facility and project needs since 2010.
  • Broad Client Portfolio: Trusted daily by a diverse range of sectors, including the hospitality sector, schools, office buildings, and property syndics.
  • Direct Leadership Involvement: Ensures short communication lines and rapid execution by routing client interactions straight through the company business manager.
